# DTPharMol

Deep molecular generation techniques have considerably expanded the chemical space accessible for drug discovery, yet challenges remain in multi-objective optimization, structural adaptability, and cross-scenario generalization. To address these issues, this study introduces DTPharMol, a fine-grained molecular generation framework that integrates ligand information with structural representations. The approach establishes a two-stage scheme for generation and optimization, organically linking chemical space exploration with target-oriented structural refinement. The two-stage generation-optimization design seamlessly connects chemical space exploration with target-oriented structural refinement while supporting integrated modeling of molecular diversity enhancement, physicochemical property modulation, scaffold retention, pharmacophore consistency, and docking-guided optimization. DTPharMol combines atom-level diffusion modeling with Transformer-based representation learning. A reinforcement learning-driven feedback mechanism provides an effective decoupling between global exploration and local optimization, while a scaffold-similarity filtering module broadens structural coverage. Comprehensive experiments confirm that DTPharMol delivers superior performance across multiple dimensions—including molecular diversity, property control, scaffold and pharmacophore fidelity, and docking effectiveness—thereby demonstrating its efficiency, robustness, and scalability under diverse constraints and target-oriented settings.
